Is the Gerson Therapy just juicing?

Fresh juices are the best-known part of Dr. Max Gerson's method, which is why the whole therapy is often reduced to them. Here is what he actually described.

Reviewed by the Gerson Institute Europe team Updated 8 October 2026 6 min read Also in Magyar · Deutsch
The short answer

No. Fresh juices are an important part of Dr. Gerson's original method, but only one part. His therapy is a complete daily programme: organic, salt-free plant-based meals, freshly pressed juices throughout the day, detoxification routines, specific supplements and rest, followed with qualified guidance and alongside conventional medical care.

Where the idea comes from

Glasses of bright carrot and apple juice are the image most people associate with the Gerson name. Online, that picture has turned into “Gerson juicing”: a few days of juice as a cleanse. That is not the method Dr. Gerson developed.

What Dr. Gerson actually described

Dr. Gerson first developed his dietary approach in Germany in the 1920s.[2][3] In A Cancer Therapy: Results of Fifty Cases (1958) he set out a structured daily regimen in which juice is one element among several.[1] The main parts are:

  • Organic, salt-free mealsThree plant-based meals a day, freshly prepared
  • Fresh-pressed juicesMade throughout the day and drunk straight away, not stored
  • Detoxification routinesPractical routines that support the body's own elimination
  • Supplements and restSpecific supplements as described in the protocol, and plenty of rest

Each part depends on the others. Taking only the juices out of the programme leaves something that is no longer the Gerson Therapy.

Why it is not a “cleanse”

A cleanse is usually short and done alone. Dr. Gerson's method is a lifestyle followed over a long period, with a strict daily structure, and it is demanding for the person and their family. That is why we teach it hands-on, in one-week sessions, and keep supporting people once they are home.

Complementary, not alternative

If you are considering the Gerson Therapy, please talk to your doctor first. Our programme works alongside conventional medical care, and we encourage you to keep your doctors informed and involved.

What the research says

Scientific research on the Gerson Therapy is limited, and it has not been tested in large controlled clinical trials.[3] We say this openly because we believe people deserve clear information, not promises.

What this means for you

If you are interested in the Gerson lifestyle, start with the whole picture: what a normal day looks like, what equipment you need, and how it fits with your medical care. That is exactly what our one-week sessions cover.

Sources
