Questions

Before you ask

Can I continue my current medical treatment?

Yes. Our programme is complementary, never a replacement for conventional medical care. We encourage you to keep your doctors informed and involved, and the session includes guidance on how to do that.

Is the session right for everyone?

Not always. Before every session we look at each person's situation individually, and we will tell you honestly if the session or the therapy may not be suitable for you.

How long is the Gerson Therapy followed at home?

It is a long-term commitment, often two years or more, depending on the person. The one-week session teaches you how to continue at home, and our online guidance supports you along the way.

Can a family member or carer come with me?

Please ask us. A family member who understands the daily routine makes life at home much easier, so we are happy to discuss the options with you.

Do I need to speak Hungarian?

No. Sessions are held in Hungarian, English or German, and we can discuss other languages.

What does a session cost?

We send you a clear, written quote before you book, so you know exactly what is included.
