Before you read on

Looking for an alternative to chemotherapy?

Please read this first.

Many people find us after a difficult diagnosis, hoping for an alternative to chemotherapy or other medical treatment. We understand that wish, and we take it seriously. But the Gerson Therapy, as we practise it, is not an alternative to medical treatment. It is a complementary lifestyle programme, followed alongside the care your doctors recommend.

  • ✓Please never stop or refuse a treatment your doctors recommend because of anything you read here.
  • ✓Talk to your doctors first, and keep them informed and involved.
  • ✓Then talk to us: we will explain honestly how the Gerson lifestyle can fit alongside your care.

The Gerson Therapy

Not a juice cleanse. A complete, structured way of living.

The basic principle is simple: to nourish the body and to support its detoxification. Four closely connected parts work together.

01

Organic, salt-free food

Fresh salads, oatmeal, Hippocrates soup, jacket potatoes and fresh fruit, all organic and prepared without salt.

02

Fresh-pressed juices

Carrot, apple and green-leaf juices, pressed throughout the day and drunk straight away.

03

Supporting detoxification

Practical routines that support the body's own elimination, taught step by step.

04

Guidance & rhythm

Supplements as the protocol describes, a clear daily schedule and a trained team beside you.

Our approach

We follow Dr. Gerson's original method.

Dr. Max Gerson was a German physician. Searching for relief from his own severe migraines, he developed a salt-free, plant-based diet, and later saw it help patients with other conditions too. Over 30 years he refined it into the therapy described in his 1958 book.

Over the years, members of the Gerson family each carried his work forward a little differently. Our centre goes back to the source: the therapy as Dr. Max Gerson himself developed and documented it.
