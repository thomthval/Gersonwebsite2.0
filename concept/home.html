Our principles

Six commitments behind everything we do

They shape every session, every article on this site and every answer we give.

Complementary, not alternative

The Gerson lifestyle is offered alongside the care your doctors recommend, never as a replacement for it.

Evidence-informed, not promise-driven

We explain what is known, cite our sources and never promise outcomes.

Personalised, not one-size-fits-all

Every programme is adapted to the person's health, circumstances and everyday life.

In cooperation with conventional medical care

We encourage you to keep your doctors informed and involved at every step.

Focused on the whole person and lifestyle

Food, rest, daily rhythm and emotional wellbeing, not a single symptom.

Safety and transparency first

Clear information about what the programme involves, what it costs and when it may not be suitable.

The Gerson Therapy

Not a juice cleanse. A complete, structured way of living.

Dr. Max Gerson developed the therapy in the 1920s–50s. These are its foundations, explained in plain language, with sources for every claim.

01

Organic, plant-based food

Freshly prepared, salt-free meals built around organic vegetables, fruit and whole grains.

02

Fresh-pressed juices

Prepared throughout the day from organic produce with the right equipment, as the protocol describes.

03

Supporting detoxification

Practical routines that support the body's own elimination, taught step by step.

04

Guidance & rhythm

Daily schedules, supplements per protocol and a trained team, so the lifestyle is sustainable at home.

Myths & facts

A lot is said about Gerson. Here's what the sources actually say.

Each article answers one common question and cites the original books, so search engines and readers find the accurate version first.

Common claim

“The Gerson Therapy is basically drinking a lot of juice.”

What the sources say

Juices are one part of a whole daily programme that also covers diet, detoxification, supplements and rest.

Common claim

“Every programme called ‘Gerson’ follows the same protocol.”

What the sources say

Many programmes use the name. Accredited centres work to the protocol and training set by the Gerson Institute.
