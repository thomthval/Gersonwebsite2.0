# Design notes: why the new site looks the way it does

These notes are for presenting the concept. Each section of the home page is listed in order, with the reason it is there. After that come the things that were left out on purpose, and the open questions only your mother and Dr. Nagy Melánia can answer.

## The idea in one sentence

A calm, honest, warm site that keeps the look people already know (cream, leaf green, the lotus, the Barlow typeface). It says clearly that Gerson is **complementary, not an alternative**, and it puts the real people and the real house front and centre.

## Movement (what moves, and why it stays calm)

- Headline words rise in one by one. The house photo slowly zooms ("Ken Burns"). The small photos and the location chip float gently.
- As you scroll, sections and cards fade and slide in, one after another.
- The menu shrinks, and a thin progress line runs along the top of the screen.
- The two timelines (a Gerson day, and Dr. Gerson's life) fill in as you scroll. On the Sessions page, the seven days light up one by one.
- Cards lift and photos zoom slightly under the mouse, and the photo strip scrolls continuously.
- Nothing flashes, there is no sound and no autoplay video. Visitors who switch off animations in their device settings get a still page. The tone stays gentle, because many visitors are going through a hard time.

## Languages

- Every page exists in English, Hungarian, German and French. The switch keeps you on the same page in the new language.
- Hungarian uses the formal "Ön", as the current site does. Names are in Hungarian order on the Hungarian pages (Dr. Nagy Melánia, Borsa Noémi) and in Western order elsewhere.
- The translations are a careful first draft. A native speaker should read the medical wording in each language before anything goes live.
- On the real site each language gets its own address (gerson.hu/hu/, /de/, /fr/). That is what lets Google show the right language in each country.

## Left out on purpose (and why)

- **Healing testimonials and "cure" wording.** These conflict with principle 2 (evidence-informed, not promise-driven), and EU and Hungarian rules restrict health claims. Personal stories can live on a separate, carefully worded "Stories" page if wanted.
- **The team's own diagnoses.** The current site mentions them. On the front page they read like medical claims, so the bios here focus on roles and qualifications. The founders line just says they "know the therapy from their own lives".
- **Coffee enemas by name.** The front page says "detoxification routines". The details belong in an article with proper context, not in a headline.
- **Prices.** They are "on request, with a clear written quote", until your mother decides what to publish.
- **The long Dr. Gerson biography.** It is shortened to a paragraph and a timeline. The full text can become its own article.
- **The old slider ("Our services / Accreditation / Treatment options").** Its content is now in the counters, the sessions block and the care note.
- **A separate gallery page.** It is replaced by the "Where the sessions take place" section and the photo strip. A full gallery can still be added.

## Open questions for your mother (the last 10%)

1. **Spelling:** the current site says **Dr. Nagy Melánia**; you wrote "Melinda". The mockup uses Melánia.
2. **Beata Bishop:** is co-founder still the right way to present her today?
3. **Noémi as first contact:** is she happy to be shown as the person who reads every inquiry?
4. **Team:** is the list current? The portraits are from 2020; new photos would lift the page a lot.
5. **Accreditation wording:** the current site says "first and only". The Gerson Institute now lists three licensed clinics, so the mockup says "first". Confirm the exact wording with the Gerson Institute.
6. **Facts that differ between the Hungarian and English pages of the current site:**
   - the address postcode (1022 vs 1021)
   - the company registration number (13-09-383370 vs 13-09-123640)
   - the tax number (…-2-13 vs …-2-41)
   - the reply time (4 vs 2 working days; the mockup says two)
7. **Sessions:**
   - real dates
   - what is actually included
   - which languages are offered (is French possible?)
   - whether companions can come
   - the online course details
   - whether the "Gerson day" and the day-by-day week match what really happens
8. **Photos:** which ones show the current house in Pilisborosjenő, and which show the earlier venue? The exercise photo and some room photos may be from the old house.
9. **Article wording**, especially the honest "What the research says" paragraph.
10. **Facebook:** the Gerson Europe Center Facebook page can't be read without logging in. Three to five favourite posts or topics would make good first journal articles.
11. **Logo:** a higher-resolution file. The current one is 109×109 px.
